BLIT386BLASTER

By Justin Johnson

SERVER WAKE-UP

The multiplayer server starts on demand. If nobody has played for a while it is asleep, and waking it can take about a minute. Meanwhile you can fly solo; the game joins everyone else as soon as the server is up (the bottom bar shows OFFLINE until then).

CONTROLS

  • Click the game, then click or press Space to launch
  • W S thrust toward / away from the crosshair
  • A D strafe
  • Mouse to aim, left click or Space to fire
  • R on the title screen for a new name and color

RULES

Rocks take three hits per size and split twice. The smallest pieces drop red health circles that refill empty health. Enemy hits cost one circle; each kill adds a new one (up to 10). But every kill also slows you a little, and your ship doubles in size at 5 kills and triples at 10, so leaders make big targets. When you die, your kills go on a leaderboard that forgets scores after five minutes.

PRIVATE ROOMS

Add ?room=yourname to the address to play in your own room. Anyone who opens the same address joins you. Letters, numbers, - and _ only.

TWO PLAYERS, ONE BROWSER

Tabs share your saved name, so they would be the same pilot. Add ?profile=2 for a second identity: open a second player. Put the windows side by side; browsers pause games in hidden tabs.
